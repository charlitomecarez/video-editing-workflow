## Editing notes (from style)

- **Pacing:** ~1.7s per shot (about 194 cuts total)
- **Captions:** No full subtitles on screen. Keywords, logos, code snippets and short labels pop in as they're said
- **Music:** Low, steady background track (around -28 dB). The voiceover leads
- **Transitions:** Hard cuts on the word. No fades, no stacking
- **Color:** Dark background (#0d1117-ish) with bright accent colors and high-contrast text
- **Visuals:** Something new every 1-2 s (0.8 s in the cold open). Kinetic captions on charcoal (template caption): 1-4 key words cut exactly on the spoken word, sometimes with a face or logo inside the sentence or written over a photo. Stats, photos and receipts fill the whole screen (full-frame graphics, no floating cards). A 0.5-1 s reaction clip after a joke lands (placeholder until you add it). Never the presenter's face
- **Sound effects:** Subtle pops or whooshes on element entrances, an occasional comedic sound on jokes. Use sparingly
