## Editing notes (from style)

- **Pacing:** ~1.7 s per shot. Something new every 1-2 s (0.8 s in the cold open)
- **Captions:** none on screen. Keywords pop in as they're said (kinetic captions on charcoal)
- **Music:** low, steady bed under the voice (around -28 dB)
- **Transitions:** hard cuts on the word. No fades
- **Visuals:** stats and photos fill the whole screen. Never your face
- **Sound effects:** 16 cues in ~3 min (~5/min), 3 comedic ones at least 15 s apart (record-scratch, sad-trombone, rimshot). None on the deadpan line (row 28) or the CTA
