# Hooks Library

The first 1-3 seconds decide whether people keep watching. Swap in your topic for the brackets.

## Patterns
| Pattern | Template | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Result first** | "This is [result]. Here's how." | "This meal cost ₱80. Here's how." |
| **Mistake** | "Stop [common action]." | "Stop washing rice like this." |
| **Question** | "Why does [surprising thing] happen?" | "Why does your phone video look blurry on TikTok?" |
| **Bold claim** | "[Thing] is a waste of money." | "Expensive editing apps are a waste of money." |
| **Curiosity gap** | "Nobody talks about [thing]." | "Nobody talks about this Kdenlive setting." |
| **Challenge** | "I tried [thing] for [time]." | "I ate on ₱100 a day for a week." |
| **List** | "[N] [things] I wish I knew before [X]." | "3 things I wish I knew before freelancing." |
| **Comparison** | "₱50 vs ₱500 [thing]." | "₱50 vs ₱500 earphones." |
| **POV / relatable** | "POV: [relatable moment]." | "POV: payday is 3 days away." |
| **Direct callout** | "If you [specific situation], watch this." | "If you edit on a laptop, watch this." |
| **Story** | "[Unexpected event], and it changed [thing]." | "My client rejected my edit, and it changed how I cut." |

## Visual hooks (pair with the line)
- Start mid-action. Skip the walk-up and setup.
- Show the end result in the first frame.
- Movement toward the camera, or a fast punch-in zoom
- Big on-screen text repeating the hook, since many viewers watch muted
- A pattern break: unexpected prop, location or sound

## Rules
- No "Hi guys, welcome back" before the hook.
- The hook must be honest. The video has to deliver what it promises, or retention drops.
- Test it by asking yourself: would *you* stop scrolling for this?
- Write 3 hooks per video (`[script].hook_options`), then pick the strongest or film two and compare.

## Call-to-action patterns
- "Comment [specific word] and I'll [specific thing]."
- "Part 2 if this gets [N] comments."
- "Save this for your next [situation]."
- "Follow for [specific promise], every [day]."
