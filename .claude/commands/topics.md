Generate video topic ideas for: $ARGUMENTS

1. Read `topics/niches.json`, `topics/ideas.md` (if it exists) and the posted scripts in `video-scripts/` so you don't repeat past ideas.
2. Come up with 10 ideas (or the count given). For each one, give:
   - A title (short and curiosity-driven)
   - The best style key from `styles/styles.json`
   - A one-line hook
   - Why it could perform well
3. Mix formats: tips, challenges, comparisons, stories, myths.
4. Ask which ones to keep, then append them to `topics/ideas.md` as `- [ ] [niche] Title (style)` under today's date.
