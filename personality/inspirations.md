## Study method
1. Pick one video and transcribe it: `tools/subtitles.sh references/<video>.mp4`
2. Mark every laugh in the transcript and label the technique (see the toolkit in `bestfriend.md`).
3. Count them: how many per minute? Where are they: in the setup, in the visuals, in asides?
4. Note what the visuals do during the joke (`python3 tools/analyze_cuts.py ... --frames`).
5. Write down 1-2 techniques in "What I like" above, then practice them in your own words on your own topic.
